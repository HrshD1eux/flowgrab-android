Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Direct answers to the most common questions about FlowGrab.

What is FlowGrab and how does it work? ▼
FlowGrab is a free, open-source Android video and audio downloader powered by yt-dlp and Material Design 3. It runs native yt-dlp, FFmpeg, and aria2c binaries on your Android device to scrape video streams, download fragments in parallel, and cleanly remux them into MP4, MKV, MP3, OPUS, or FLAC without any server-side middlemen or quality loss.
How do I install FlowGrab APK on my Android device? ▼
1. Tap Download APK above (choose Universal if you're not sure which processor your device has).
2. Open the downloaded .apk file in your browser or file manager.
3. When prompted by Android, enable "Allow from this source" in settings.
4. Tap Install and open the app.
Which architecture APK should I download? ▼
• ARM64 (arm64-v8a): Best for almost all modern Android phones produced since 2018 (Google Pixel, Samsung Galaxy, OnePlus, Xiaomi).
• Universal: Works on 100% of Android devices running Android 8.0 or newer. Choose this if in doubt.
• ARM32 (armeabi-v7a): For older Android phones and budget Android Go hardware.
• x86_64: For Android emulators (BlueStacks, LDPlayer) and Chromebooks.
How does FlowGrab bypass YouTube's download speed limit? ▼
YouTube restricts automated web downloads to 40–70 KB/s via SABR throttling and Botguard checks. FlowGrab embeds intelligent client profile spoofing (simulating Android Creator and TV clients) combined with aria2c multi-thread chunking to bypass this limit and download at full bandwidth speed.
Can I extract YouTube audio to MP3 or OPUS? ▼
Yes. FlowGrab provides dedicated audio downloading with options for OPUS (best efficiency and fidelity), MP3 320kbps CBR (maximum universal compatibility), M4A, FLAC, and WAV. It embeds album art thumbnails and ID3 artist/title metadata automatically.
Is FlowGrab completely free, safe, and open source? ▼
Yes. FlowGrab is 100% free and open-source under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3). There are no ads, trackers, paywalls, or third-party SDKs. You can inspect the complete source code on GitHub.
